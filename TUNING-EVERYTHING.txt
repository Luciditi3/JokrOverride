JOKR OVERRIDE - WHAT TO TUNE
============================

Tune in this order. Do not tune PID to hide a mechanical, sensor, or geometry
problem. Use a clear practice area, keep people clear, and have a spotter ready
to disable the robot.


1. VERIFY THE ROBOT
-------------------
Before any tuning, verify motor ports/directions, gearing, wheel size, tire
condition, tracker contact, and IMU mounting. Current drivetrain:
  Left: -8, -2, -1 (blue)
  Right: 3, 9, 10 (blue)
  IMU: 18
  Vertical tracking wheel: port -17; horizontal tracker is disabled

Measure actual robot travel with a tape. If encoder distance is consistently
wrong, correct `wheel_distance_in` in src/rw-config.cpp first. Confirm the
robot's turn direction and IMU reading; measure/correct `imu_scale` only if a
full physical turn does not read approximately 360 degrees. Then check
odometry against measured movement before trusting field-coordinate paths.


2. RUN THE DRIVE PID AUTOTUNER
------------------------------
`autonomous()` in src/main.cpp is currently set to `runDriveAutotune()`, so no
source edit is needed to start tuning. Use a practice session, not a match.
The routine needs clear space for repeated
24-inch drives and 90-degree turns. It alternates forward/reverse drive trials
and clockwise/counterclockwise turns instead of turning and driving back to a
start point after each trial. The phases tune linear drive and velocity first,
turning second, and heading correction last. In autonomous, press A to start
the PID search; press B on the controller or disable the robot to abort. It
tunes:
  distance_kp, distance_kd
  turn_kp, turn_kd
  heading_correction_kp, heading_correction_kd
  drive_velocity_kP

Integral gains are not part of this search; leave them unchanged. The tuner
performs repeated trials and final validation. Treat a review/failure result as
not ready for competition; check setup, consistency, and the reported gains
before trying again.
There is a 250 ms settling pause between motion trials. Drive and turn checks
use 0.5 in and 1 degree, matching RW's built-in motion arrival tolerances;
those tolerances are fixed inside the RW library and are not independently
adjustable by this project's autotuner.

The robot must be enabled in autonomous for the tuner to start. Keep a spotter
at the disable control and ensure the robot has room to drive and turn. After
tuning, change `autonomous()` back to the desired match routine before using
the robot in a match.

To preview the realistic search in RW's simulator from a neutral starting pose,
run:
  wsl sh sim/run-sim.sh --autotune --start "0,0,0" --seed 1
The simulator-only baseline uses Kp=1, Ki=0, Kd=0 for drive/turn, a strong
heading correction seed, and velocity Kp=0. It reports each candidate and
writes a replay. Simulated values are not hardware settings.

The tuner saves validated settings to `rw_drive_tuning.txt` on the Brain SD
card and loads them at startup. Without a working SD card, copy the displayed
values into src/rw-config.cpp.


3. MEASURE DRIVE FEEDFORWARD (OPTIONAL)
---------------------------------------
Run `runDriveAutotune()` again and press X for the kS/kV/kA calibration. Clear
at least 3 feet in front and behind the robot. It drives forward and backward
in short voltage steps; cancel or disable to stop it. Do not run this if the
area is not clear.

These are already shown in the autotuner's results:
  drive_kS: voltage needed to start moving
  drive_kV: voltage per inch/second
  drive_kA: voltage per inch/second squared

When calibration passes its checks, the values are saved with the PID settings
to SD. Otherwise, leave drive_kV and drive_kA at 0 so RW estimates them, and
do not replace the current values with a failed measurement.


4. TUNE ONLY WHAT STILL LOOKS WRONG
-----------------------------------
Profiles and paths: if those routines are used and still slip or miss after
the drive tuning, adjust one setting at a time in src/rw-config.cpp:
  profile_max_accel       lower to reduce wheel slip
  profile_max_speed       set only to override the automatic speed
  profile_turn_speed /
  profile_turn_accel      adjust profiled turns if needed
  path_lookahead          smaller tracks tighter; larger is smoother
  path_max_lateral_accel  lower to slow down in curves

Driver feel: adjust `driver_curve` or `driver_deadband` based on driver
practice, not autonomous results.

Mechanisms: tune `lift_positions` only after measuring safe preset angles.
Then adjust `lift_kp`, `lift_kd`, or `lift_hold_mv` one at a time if the lift
misses, overshoots, or sags. Verify intake and side-toggle direction and
clearance before changing their behavior.

Sensors: distance-sensor offsets are currently zero placeholders. Measure and
set the actual offsets before enabling wall-based odometry resets. MCL is not
started by the current pre-autonomous code, so do not tune MCL for this robot.


5. VERIFY THE MATCH ROUTINE
---------------------------
Test the selected routine in src/main.cpp from its real starting position.
Repeat the full routine at least three times; record where it misses, slips,
or runs out of time. Tune that segment or mechanism only, then repeat. Do not
change multiple settings between runs.

Relevant files:
  src/main.cpp          selects the autonomous routine
  src/drive-autotune.cpp PID/feedforward tuning and SD settings
  src/rw-config.cpp     drivetrain geometry and RW settings
  src/lift.cpp          lift gains and preset positions
  src/rw-autons.cpp     autonomous routines
